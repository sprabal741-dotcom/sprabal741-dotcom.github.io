Target field

Clinical research or regulatory affairs

Next degree

Master’s in Germany, in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Regulatory Affairs or Clinical Research

Why clinical research and regulatory affairs

Pharmaceutical companies need people who run drug trials and people who check that medicines meet legal standards before they reach patients. Both jobs sit between the science and the public, and that is where I want to work.

Why Germany

  • Public universities charge very low tuition or none, which makes it far more affordable than the US.
  • It is a major pharma hub, home to companies such as Bayer, Merck and Boehringer Ingelheim.
  • After a Master’s, an 18-month job-seeker visa gives time to find work.
  • Industry starting salaries are roughly €45,000 to €60,000 a year, and rise with experience.

My plan

  1. Finish B.Pharm with strong grades, since many universities look closely at CGPA.
  2. Learn German to at least A2/B1, which helps with both jobs and daily life.
  3. Prepare for IELTS or TOEFL, and the GRE if a university asks for it.
  4. Apply for an MS in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Regulatory Affairs or Clinical Research.
  5. Do an internship during the Master’s, then look for a full-time job.
